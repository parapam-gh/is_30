The Afrosiyob

High-speed train connecting all three cities. Clean, fast, tea service included. Tickets open exactly 45 days before departure — at midnight Tashkent time (UTC+5). They sell out in hours. Book at railway.uz. Name must match your passport exactly. Business class: +$10. Worth it.

Can't get Afrosiyob? → Sharq train. Slower by ~1h. Gets you there.

[ MONEY ]

Uzbek sum. Cards work in restaurants and hotels. Bazaars are cash only. Bring some euros/dollars, change when you arrive.

[ GETTING AROUND ]

Yandex Go. Download it before you leave. Add your card. Works everywhere on the route. Easier than explaining addresses. Cheaper than arguing about prices.

[ WATER ]

Bottled. Always. 3–4 litres per day in the heat. This is not a suggestion.

[ SIM CARD ]

Ucell or Beeline. Buy at Tashkent airport arrivals. Around $5–10. Works everywhere on the route.

[ MOSQUES ]

Covered shoulders, long bottoms. Headscarves for women — they'll give you one at the entrance for free. We will be going to a lot of mosques. This is a feature, not a bug.

[ PACK THIS ]

Light, pale clothing (40°C — dark colours are a tactical error) · Hat, non-negotiable · SPF50+ · Flat comfortable shoes (cobblestones everywhere) · Swimwear for the hammam · Imodium + rehydration salts (just in case — the plov is worth it) · Cash in sums
